## 1. Data Classification Thresholds

### 1.1 Pelvic Assessment Lógica
The system processes pelvic measurements from the lateral and anterior views:
- **Pelvic Retroversion:** If the measured angle is $\approx$ 5.5° or higher, the system flags a primary "Protection and Holding" pattern in the lumbar region.
- **Pelvic Elevation:** If the lateral tilt exceeds $\approx$ 2.0°, it is classified as an asymmetric redistribution of load, prompting a pelvic-scapular compensation check.

### 1.2 Cervical Axis Lógica
- **Cervical Retraction:** Angles $\approx$ 7.0° or higher are translated as protective head retraction (defensive antenna).
- **Cervical Protrusion:** Angles $\approx$ -6.0° or lower indicate forward projection under excessive cognitive load.
