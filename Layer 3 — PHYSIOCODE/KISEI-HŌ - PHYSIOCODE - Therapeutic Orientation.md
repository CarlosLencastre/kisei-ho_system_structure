## 2. Therapeutic Orientation (Step 10)
**This step has been updated to v1.1 with Kyo/Jitsu balancing.**
Provide non-prescriptive movement orientations:- Suggest qualities (e.g., grounding, centring, breathing)- Avoid techniques or instructions- Align with client's energetic and structural state- **Provide Kyo/Jitsu balancing orientations**- **Align BTL and Flow State patterns with movement qualities**
### Kyo/Jitsu Balancing Principles
**For Jitsu (excess) patterns:**- Movement qualities: softening, releasing, allowing- Avoid: intense, forceful, activating movements- Goal: reduce congestion, allow energy to move
**For Kyo (deficiency) patterns:**- Movement qualities: gentle support, gradual activation, nurturing- Avoid: aggressive, depleting movements- Goal: build energy, support function gradually
**For combined Kyo-Jitsu patterns:**- Prioritize: Usually soften Jitsu areas first, then support Kyo areas- Balance: Allow upper areas to release while lower areas gradually activate

| BTL Pattern | Movement Quality Suggestions ||-------------|------------------------------|| Anterior BTL dominant | "allow breath to move freely through the chest," "soften the front of the body" || Posterior BTL dominant | "notice the back of the body," "allow the spine to feel supported" || Lateral BTL tension | "find balance between left and right," "allow the side body to lengthen" || Spiral BTL tension | "notice rotation in the spine," "allow the body to find easy turning" |
