## 1. Introduction: Your Body and Movement
Based on the unique way your body is currently organized, you may explore gentle movements that support your natural reorganization. These suggestions are invitations to reconnect with your physical presence, allowing balance to emerge naturally.

This program is tailored to:
*   Support your body’s adaptive pathways.
*   Encourage gentle, non-corrective somatic release.
*   Offer sensory-driven, comfortable movement qualities.

## 2. Segmental Movement Exploration

### A. The Upper Region (Areas of Held Presence)
In the upper area of your trunk, where tension or a sense of over-accumulation may show itself:
*   You may explore soft movements along your back. This allows your spine to find stable support without force or strain.
*   In the chest area, you can invite movements that allow softening. This may support your breathing and help your internal flow to move more naturally.

### B. The Lower Region (Areas of Gentle Support)
In the lower part of your body, where presence may feel less active or in need of quiet nourishment:
*   Gentle movements that offer support without demanding effort may help your body find greater vitality.
*   Soft, grounding movements that connect you to the surface beneath you can encourage a sense of calm stability.

## 3. General Quality of Movement
*   **Pace:** Extremely slow, continuous, and soft.
*   **Rhythm:** Allow your body to find its own natural rhythm without counting repetitions, tracking sets, or forcing performance.
*   **Rule of Ease:** If a movement or position does not feel comfortable, you may simply pause, adjust, or stop. Realignment emerges as a consequence of ease, never through forced effort.
