## Exercise Execution Recommendations

* **Frequency:** Ideally, perform the stretching and mobility exercises daily or every other day.
* **Intensity:** Stretches should create a sensation of mild to moderate tension, never sharp or radiating pain. Strengthening exercises should be performed with full control, without jerky movements or holding your breath. If pain occurs, stop the exercise.
* **Breathing:** Maintain deep, slow, and controlled breathing throughout all exercises. Exhale during the effort phase (e.g., lifting, pushing, or contracting). Inhale during the release or return phase.
* **Progression:** Start with the indicated repetitions and holds. Only increase the difficulty (e.g., longer hold, more reps, less support) when you can complete the current set with perfect form and no discomfort.
