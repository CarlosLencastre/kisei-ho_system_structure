## 2. Categorization Systems (v1.1 Updated)

### A. Structural Categories
*   **grounding movements:** Grounding the base, establishing connection with the earth.
*   **centring movements:** Finding gravity and central pelvic organization.
*   **softening movements:** Releasing rigid protection, opening tissues.
*   **stabilising movements:** Securing the midline and structural support.
*   **lengthening movements:** Restoring space along myofascial pathways.
*   **breathing-based movements:** Coordinating motion with diaphragmatic rhythms.
*   **gentle mobility:** Opening joint pathways without effort.
*   **gentle expansion:** Expanding internal space outward.
*   **gentle compression:** Condensing force into a supportive axis.
*   **slow rhythmic movement:** Utilizing oscillation to dissolve rigid states.

### B. BTL-Aligned Categories (v1.1 NEW)
*   **Anterior BTL movements:** Softening front body, breath expansion through chest.
*   **Posterior BTL movements:** Lengthening along back, gentle spinal awareness.
*   **Lateral BTL movements:** Balance between sides, side body lengthening.
*   **Spiral BTL movements:** Gentle rotation, finding easy turning.

### C. Flow State Categories (v1.1 NEW)
*   **Smooth Flow movements:** Slow rhythmic movement, maintaining current state.
*   **Stagnant Flow movements:** Softening, gentle expansion, breathing support.
*   **Rebellious Flow movements:** Slow, deliberate movement, finding natural direction.
*   **Deficient Flow movements:** Gentle, nurturing movement, supporting without forcing.
*   **Excess Flow movements:** Softening movement, allowing rather than doing.

### D. Kyo/Jitsu Balancing Categories (v1.1 NEW)
*   **Jitsu-softening movements:** Gentle release, breath-supported softening.
*   **Kyo-nurturing movements:** Gentle support, breathing-based activation.
*   **Mixed Kyo-Jitsu movements:** Balance approach (soften upper then support lower, or vice versa).

## 5. Sōtai Principles Integration (v1.1 NEW)
All movement categories must integrate Sōtai principles:
*   **Natural movement toward comfort:** Guide the body away from pain and restriction toward ease and comfortable directions.
*   **Gentle correction without force:** Realignment emerges as a consequence of ease, never through forced correction.
*   **Finding easier positions:** If a movement feels heavy, seek a lighter variation.
*   **Permissive Language:** Use conditional verbs in client-facing outputs (*"pode libertar"*, *"poderá suavizar"*, *"poderia encontrar equilíbrio"*).
