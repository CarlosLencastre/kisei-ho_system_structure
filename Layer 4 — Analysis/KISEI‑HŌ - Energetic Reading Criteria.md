## Introduction
This document defines the criteria for reading energetic states within the KISEI‑HŌ method. It provides the standard vocabulary, symbolism, and rules to ensure consistent interpretation across structure, energy, and axes readings, while maintaining a non-clinical, non-therapeutic narrative.

---

## 1. Energetic States
Energetic states describe the status of Ki flow and its directional behavior. They are described in relation to structural axis and global patterns. The main states are:
- Flow: Ki moves unobstructed through the axis, indicating stability and adaptability.
- Blockage: Ki stagnates or is interrupted, indicating tension or obstruction in the region.
- Excess: Ki accumulates beyond functional levels, indicating overactivation or compensatory effort.
- Deficiency: Ki is depleted or dispersed, indicating grounding loss or fatigue.

These states are read in context with the Root → Centre → Firmament axis and are used to inform interpretation within the Integrated Analysis.

---

## 2. Energetic Symbols
- Flow: →
- Blockage: ⨂
- Excess: ◎
- Deficiency: ⊖

Symbols are used as concise markers in reports and data maps, and should be translated to narrative terms in client-facing outputs.

---

## 3. Energetic Meaning
- Flow: Indicates a coherent energetic movement with minimal resistance.
- Blockage: Represents a local or regional resistance or stagnation.
- Excess: Signals overactivation or compensatory tension.
- Deficiency: Signals depletion or collapse of energy in the region.

Interpretations must be grounded in the structural context and axis behavior.

---

## 4. Regional Energetic Reading
### Root (Enraizamento)
- Focus on grounding and base stability.
### Centre (Hara)
- Focus on internal pressure, respiratory expansion, and core organization.
### Firmament (Superior Axis)
- Focus on upper body expansion, cervical freedom, and energetic projection.

Each region is interpreted in relation to the global energy patterns and the axis flow.

---

## 5. Global Energetic Reading
- Global patterns describe the overall energy architecture (e.g., ascending, descending, spiraling tendencies).
- They integrate data across all regions to identify dominant patterns and transitions.

---

## 6. Safety Requirements
- Do not attribute psychological or emotional states to the energy reading; describe energy in neutral terms.
- Do not imply clinical or diagnostic conclusions from energetic readings; present as patterns and tendencies.
- Ensure all energetic readings align with the structural and axis readings and do not contradict them.

---

## 7. Final Principle
Energetic readings are a descriptive, neutral complement to structural readings and axis interpretations. They should inform a holistic view of the body’s adaptive organization without asserting causality or prescribing intervention.
