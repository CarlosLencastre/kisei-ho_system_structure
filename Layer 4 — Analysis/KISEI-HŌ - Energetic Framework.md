## 1. Energetic Flow Principles
**Updated to v1.1 with Flow States and Energetic Principles.**
Ki flow is interpreted through three fundamental behaviours:
| Behaviour | Definition | Notes ||-----------|------------|-------|| Flow → | Ki moves unobstructed through the axis | Indicates stability and adaptability || Blockage ⨂ | Ki stagnates or is interrupted | Indicates structural or emotional tension || Excess ◎ | Ki accumulates beyond functional levels | Indicates overactivation or compensatory effort (Jitsu) || Deficiency ⊖ | Ki is depleted or dispersed | Indicates collapse, fatigue or loss of grounding (Kyo) |
These behaviours are always interpreted in relation to the structural axis (Root → Centre → Firmament).

### Energetic Principles (v1.1 NEW)
The governing rules of energy movement, transformation, and balance:
| Principle | Description | Application ||-----------|-------------|-------------|| Yin-Yang Balance | Opposing forces that must remain in dynamic balance | Kyo and Jitsu must be balanced for optimal function || Five Elements Circulation | Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water cycle | Energy moves through element phases; blockage in one affects all || Meridian Pathway Integrity | Energy flows through defined channels | BTL pathways reflect meridian energy flow; disruption affects entire channel || Qi-Ki-Blood Relationship | Energy drives blood; blood nourishes energy | Flow States affect both Qi-Ki movement and tissue nourishment || Organ-System Interconnectedness | Organs affect each other through meridian connections | Kyo/Jitsu patterns in one organ affect connected organs and BTL pathways |

## 2. Energetic Flow States (v1.1 NEW)
**Flow States** describe the quality and movement of energy through meridian channels, assessed through pulse, observation, and symptomatology.
| Flow State | Symbol | Description | Assessment Criteria | Narrative Description ||------------|--------|-------------|---------------------|----------------------|| Smooth Flow | → | Unobstructed energy movement | Pulse: Full, regular; Tissue: Soft, responsive; Sensation: Ease | "Energy moves smoothly through the channels" || Stagnant Flow | ⨂ | Partial or complete obstruction | Pulse: Full, choppy; Tissue: Tense, rigid; Sensation: Tightness, heaviness | "Energy may be held or moving slowly in this area" || Rebellious Flow | ⨂↔ | Counter-flow or reverse movement | Pulse: Rapid, irregular; Tissue: Tense, vibrating; Sensation: Agitation, instability | "Energy seems to move against its natural direction" || Deficient Flow | ⊖ | Insufficient energy movement | Pulse: Thin, weak; Tissue: Soft, atrophic; Sensation: Cold, emptiness | "Energy movement may be reduced or weak in this area" || Excess Flow | ◎ | Congested or overactive movement | Pulse: Full, rapid; Tissue: Tense, swollen; Sensation: Fullness, heat | "Energy may feel held or accumulated in this area" |

### Flow State Assessment Integration
Flow States are assessed during:- PHYSIOCODE Data Extraction (Step 3)- PHYSIOCODE Pattern Integration (Step 6)- Therapist Session Guide (observations)
Flow States are translated via Translation Rules v1.1 for narrative outputs.

| State | Symbol | Interpretation | Kyo/Jitsu | Flow State ||-------|--------|----------------|-----------|------------|| Flow | → | Clear projection and stable orientation | Neutral | Smooth Flow || Blockage | ⨂ | Cervical tension, sensory conflict | Jitsu | Stagnant Flow || Excess | ◎ | Overextension, hypervigilance | Jitsu | Excess Flow || Deficiency | ⊖ | Dissipation, weak projection | Kyo | Deficient Flow |

## 7. Conflict Resolution Rules
When structural and energetic readings diverge:
1. **Prioritise energetic data** — energetic states reflect the body's current adaptive response2. **Validate axis behaviour** — Root → Centre → Firmament must be coherent3. **Cross-reference with Structural & Energetic Tables** — check BTL and Flow State classifications4. **Consult Flow State** — Flow State provides quality of energy movement beyond simple state5. **Document the conflict** — conflicts must be explicitly noted in the Integrated Analysis6. **Never speculate missing data** — if a region is not evaluated, omit it
