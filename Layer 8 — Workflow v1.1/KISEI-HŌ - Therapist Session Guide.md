## 2. Session Flow (v1.1 Updated)

The session is divided into 6 phases:

| Phase | Name | Purpose | BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu Integration |
|-------|------|---------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | Reception & Grounding | Establish trust and comfort | Initial observation of body organisation, BTL pattern |
| 2 | Anamnesis | Gather client history and context | **Clinical terms permitted** (to be transformed in outputs) |
| 3 | Physiocode Review | Review clinical data | BTL classification, Flow State assessment, Kyo/Jitsu identification |
| 4 | Body Reading | Observe and interpret body organization | BTL observation, Flow State observation, Kyo/Jitsu assessment |
| 5 | Integration & Reflection | Synthesize findings | BTL-Flow State-Kyo/Jitsu synthesis, therapeutic direction |
| 6 | Closure | Summarize and plan next steps | BTL/Kyo-Jitsu-aligned recommendations, gentle movement qualities |

## 3. Phase Details (v1.1 Updated)

### Phase 1: Reception & Grounding
- **Purpose:** Establish trust and comfort
- **Actions:** Greet client, explain process, ensure consent
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:** Initial observation of body organisation — how does the body hold itself? Is there upper or lower dominance? Is there visible BTL tension?
- **Language:** Use PT-PT or EN based on client preference (per anamnesis)

### Phase 2: Anamnesis
- **Purpose:** Gather client history and context
- **Actions:** Ask open-ended questions about body experience
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:** Gather information about energy patterns, flow, warmth/cold, fullness/emptiness
- **Clinical Terms:** **Clinical, biomechanical, diagnostic, and emotional terms ARE permitted in the anamnesis** — these will be transformed via Translation Rules before appearing in any narrative output
- **Language:** Use PT-PT or EN based on client preference

### Phase 3: Physiocode Review
- **Purpose:** Review clinical data
- **Actions:** Analyze Physiocode & Anamnesis PDF
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:** 
  - Identify BTL involvement (Anterior, Posterior, Lateral, Spiral)
  - Assess Flow States in major regions
  - Identify Kyo/Jitsu patterns
  - Determine BTL tension level
  - Cross-reference with Structural & Energetic Tables v1.1

### Phase 4: Body Reading
- **Purpose:** Observe and interpret body organization
- **Actions:** Apply Structural & Energetic Tables v1.1 and Energetic Framework v1.1
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:**
  - Observe BTL pathways — which BTL is dominant? Where are junctions?
  - Observe Flow States — which regions show which Flow States?
  - Assess Kyo/Jitsu — which regions show deficiency (Kyo) or excess (Jitsu)?
  - Cross-check observations with Physiocode data
  - Integrate Oriental Structural Terminology where appropriate (meridian correlations, Five Element dynamics)

### Phase 5: Integration & Reflection
- **Purpose:** Synthesize findings
- **Actions:** Create Integrated Analysis v1.1, discuss with client
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:**
  - Synthesize all patterns (structural, BTL, Flow States, Kyo/Jitsu)
  - Identify dominant pattern type
  - Determine therapeutic priority
  - Prepare BTL/Kyo-Jitsu-aligned recommendations
  - Transform findings for client discussion (via Translation Rules)

### Phase 6: Closure
- **Purpose:** Summarize and plan next steps
- **Actions:** Deliver Therapeutic Plan v1.1, discuss movement qualities, answer questions
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu:**
  - Present BTL/Kyo-Jitsu-aligned therapeutic direction
  - Suggest movement qualities (BTL-aligned, Flow State-appropriate, Kyo/Jitsu balancing)
  - Follow Sōtai principles (natural movement toward comfort)
  - Use conditional language

## 5. Behavioural Principles (v1.1 Updated)

The therapist must:
- Remain calm, grounded, and neutral
- Use descriptive, non-clinical language in client interactions
- Avoid diagnosis, correction, or prescription
- Follow Narrative & Persona Guidelines v1.1 (persona, tone, BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu descriptions)
- Use PT-PT for technical documentation (therapist reference)
- Use PT-PT or EN for client outputs based on client preference
- Integrate Oriental Structural Terminology where appropriate
- Apply Sōtai principles (gentle, natural movement toward comfort)

## 6. Safety Validation Steps (v1.1 Updated)

Ensure compliance with Safety & Compliance v1.1 at each phase of the session.

### Phase 1: Reception & Grounding
- **Check:** Client comfort and consent
- **Validate:** No clinical/biomechanical terms used in greeting
- **BTL check:** Initial observation — any visible BTL tension?

### Phase 2: Anamnesis
- **Check:** Avoid leading questions (e.g., "Do you have pain?" → "How do you experience your body?")
- **Validate:** Language remains neutral and body-centred
- **Clinical Terms:** Clinical terms ARE permitted here — they will be transformed later
- **Kyo/Jitsu check:** Observe language about warmth/cold, fullness/emptiness

### Phase 3: Physiocode Review
- **Check:** Confirm data integrity (no missing/malformed fields)
- **Validate:** All classifications align with Structural & Energetic Tables v1.1
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu check:** Verify BTL, Flow State, and Kyo/Jitsu classifications are complete

### Phase 4: Body Reading
- **Check:** Cross-check observations with Energetic Framework v1.1
- **Validate:** No speculative or diagnostic language
- **BTL check:** Identify BTL pathways, tension level, junctions
- **Flow State check:** Assess Flow States in major regions
- **Kyo/Jitsu check:** Identify deficiency/excess patterns

### Phase 5: Integration & Reflection
- **Check:** Ensure narrative aligns with Narrative & Persona Guidelines v1.1
- **Validate:** No forbidden terms (see Safety & Compliance v1.1)
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu check:** Verify synthesis is coherent, no contradictions

### Phase 6: Closure
- **Check:** Confirm client understanding (no clinical jargon in explanation)
- **Validate:** Tone remains calm and sensory
- **BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu check:** Verify recommendations are BTL-aligned, Flow State-appropriate, Kyo/Jitsu balancing

## 7. BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu Observation Checklist (v1.1 NEW)

### BTL Observation
- [ ] Primary BTL involvement identified (Anterior, Posterior, Lateral, Spiral)?
- [ ] BTL tension level assessed (Mild, Moderate, Marked)?
- [ ] BTL junctions identified (where BTL crosses joints or other BTLs)?
- [ ] BTL correlation with structural deviations noted?
- [ ] BTL correlation with Flow States noted?
- [ ] BTL correlation with Kyo/Jitsu patterns noted?

### Flow State Observation
- [ ] Flow State assessed in upper region?
- [ ] Flow State assessed in middle region?
- [ ] Flow State assessed in lower region?
- [ ] Flow State correlation with BTL noted?
- [ ] Flow State correlation with Kyo/Jitsu noted?
- [ ] Dominant Flow State pattern identified?

### Kyo/Jitsu Observation
- [ ] Kyo (deficiency) regions identified?
- [ ] Jitsu (excess) regions identified?
- [ ] Kyo/Jitsu balance determined (Jitsu-dominant, Kyo-dominant, Mixed)?
- [ ] Kyo/Jitsu correlation with BTL noted?
- [ ] Kyo/Jitsu correlation with Flow States noted?
- [ ] Therapeutic priority based on Kyo/Jitsu balance determined?
