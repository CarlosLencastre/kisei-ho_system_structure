## 1. Purpose
This document consolidates the internal workflow, safety, and compliance rules that govern the entire KISEI‑HŌ workflow. It combines the eight-step processing pipeline, the safety language policies, and the compliance standards to ensure consistent, neutral, and safe operation of the system by both human operators and AI components.

---

## 2. Workflow Rules (8-Step Pipeline)
All outputs must follow a fixed sequence and cannot be reordered:
1. Data Extraction
2. Structural Processing
3. Energetic Processing
4. Axis Processing
5. Integrated Pattern Reading
6. Narrative Translation
7. Therapeutic Orientation
8. Safety Validation

No step may be skipped, and no step may be reordered. The PHYSIOCODE language and Anamnesis language influence how data is translated at each step while preserving neutrality.

### 2.1 Integration Points
- The pipeline must integrate with the PHYSIOCODE processing at Step 2-3 and with the Integrated Analysis at Step 5.
- The Narrative Translation at Step 6 must apply the Translation Rules to ensure client-facing outputs are non-clinical and sensory.
- Final Safety Validation at Step 8 must enforce the Safety Rules across all downstream outputs.

---

## 3. Safety Rules
### 3.1 Forbidden Language
Outputs must avoid:
- Angles, anatomy, biomechanics, medical terminology, psychological interpretations, spiritual language, prescriptive cues, or invented data.
- Any language that implies diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment.
- Any guarantee of improvement or cure.

### 3.2 Forbidden Actions
The system must never diagnose, prescribe, or teach techniques. It should avoid dramatic or moralizing language.

### 3.3 Narrative Safety
Narratives must be calm, non-alarming, non-interpretive and non-conclusive.

### 3.4 Terminology Transformation
Clinical terms appearing in anamnesis are permitted in data collection but must be transformed via Translation Rules before outputs.

---

## 4. Narrative Rules
- Tone: calm, neutral, sensory, editorial, descriptive.
- Structure: no bullet-lists or enumerations in client outputs; use short paragraphs.
- Language: accessible, non-clinical, non-prescriptive for client outputs; professional for therapist outputs with neutral terms.

---

## 5. Compliance Rules
- All outputs must align with Master Document and all related core files.
- Safety rules take precedence in case of ambiguity.
- No content may contradict other core files; cross-field traceability must be maintained.
