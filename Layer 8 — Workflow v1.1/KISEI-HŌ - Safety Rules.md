## 2. Historical Content (Superseded)

The original content included:

### Forbidden Language
- Clinical terms (posture, misalignment, scoliosis)
- Biomechanical terms (joint, spine curvature)
- Diagnostic terms (dysfunction, syndrome)
- Prescriptive terms (you must, correct this)
- Psychological terms (stress, anxiety, trauma)
- Corrective cues (fix, align, release)

### Forbidden Actions
- Diagnose or imply pathology
- Correct or suggest fixes
- Prescribe or teach techniques
- Promise results
- Dramatize or moralize
- Interpret emotions or psychological states
- Use imperative language

### Tone and Structural Rules
- Calm, grounded, sensory
- Short paragraphs (max 3–4 sentences)
- No lists and numbers
- Fluid rhythm and sensory presence
