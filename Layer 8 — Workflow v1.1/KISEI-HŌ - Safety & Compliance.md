## 2. Fundamental Principles

### 2.1 Non-Prescription

The KISEI-HŌ method does not prescribe, diagnose, or treat diseases.
All language must strictly avoid:
- Formal clinical terms
- Medical recommendations
- Specific therapeutic instructions
- Cure assertions

### 2.2 Narrative Safety

System narrative must be:
- Soft
- Non-invasive
- Non-alarmist
- Non-interpretive
- Non-conclusive

### 2.3 Emotional Neutrality

Language must avoid:
- Value judgments
- Psychological interpretations
- Deterministic causal attributions
- Deep or intrusive emotional readings

### 2.4 Advanced Oriental Chiropractic Alignment

All narratives must integrate:
- **Body Tension Lines (BTL)** — described accessibly in outputs
- **Energetic Flow States** — described in narrative form in outputs
- **Kyo/Jitsu patterns** — described without clinical terminology in outputs
- **Sōtai principles** — gentle, natural movement toward comfort
- **Energetic Principles** — supporting natural energy flow

## 3. Terminological Directives

### 3.1 Forbidden Terms in Outputs

The following terms are **PROHIBITED in all narrative outputs** (client and therapist-facing):

#### Clinical Terms
- Diagnosis, pathology, lesion, syndrome, dysfunction
- Treatment, medical intervention, prognosis
- Cure, medical therapeutics, prescription, contraindication
- Disease (use "pattern," "condition")
- Injure (use "affect," "impact")

#### Risk Terms
- Severe, critical, dangerous, urgent, alarming

#### Guarantee Terms
- "Will improve," "will cure," "will resolve"
- "Guaranteed," "assured"

#### Corrective Terms
- "Fix," "align," "correct," "release"
- (Use "find ease," "soften," "restore naturally")

#### Performance Terms
- "Strengthen," "stretch," "improve posture"
- "Biomechanics," "trauma release," "energy healing"

**Note:** These terms ARE permitted in the **anamnesis** (clinical data collection). The Translation Rules pipeline transforms these terms into narrative-safe language for outputs.

### 3.2 Forbidden Terms: Transformation Examples

| Anamnesis (Permitted) | Narrative Output (Transformed) |
|-----------------------|-------------------------------|
| "Herniated disc L4-L5" | "patterns of tension in the lower back" |
| "Chronic sciatica" | "long-standing patterns of lower body tension" |
| "Scoliosis 15°" | "a lateral tendency in the spine" |
| "Joint dysfunction" | "reduced movement quality in the joint" |
| "Treat this condition" | "support this pattern" |

### 3.3 Allowed Terms

#### Neutral and Structural Terms
- Pattern, adaptation, body organisation, flow, tension
- Comfort, space, movement, postural reference

#### Energetic Terms (v1.1 NEW)
- Ki, flow, field, interior landscape, baseline
- Centre, Hara, energy movement
- **Flow States:** smooth movement, held energy, reduced movement
- **BTL:** line of tension, tension pathway
- **Kyo/Jitsu:** more active, less active, fuller, weaker

#### Narrative Optional Phrases
- "The body shows..."
- "The body has learned..."
- "The body adapted..."
- "The body found a form..."
- "The body requests space..."
- "Energy may move..."
- "Tension may soften..."

## 4. Language and Reframing Rules

### 4.1 Causal Attribution
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "Anxiety causes cervical tension."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "Cervical tension and anxiety seem to be part of the same pattern."

### 4.2 Psychological Interpretation
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "The client represses emotions."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "The body shows zones of tension that may reflect internal patterns."

### 4.3 Result Promise
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "Dizziness will disappear."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "The body may find lighter ways to organise itself."

### 4.4 Clinical vs. Descriptive Language
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "There is vestibular system dysfunction."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "The body may lose postural reference during certain transitions."

### 4.5 BTL-Descriptive Language (v1.1 NEW)
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "Posterior BTL tension at C3-C4 with marked restriction."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "Tension accumulates along the back, particularly in the neck region."

### 4.6 Flow State Descriptive Language (v1.1 NEW)
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "Excess Flow (◎) in upper body with Rebellious Flow pattern."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "Energy may feel held or accumulated in the upper body, with movement against its natural direction."

### 4.7 Kyo/Jitsu Descriptive Language (v1.1 NEW)
- ❌ **Incorrect:** "Marked Jitsu pattern in upper chest with Kyo in lower abdomen."
- ✔️ **Correct:** "The upper region may feel fuller or warmer while the lower region may feel cooler or weaker."
