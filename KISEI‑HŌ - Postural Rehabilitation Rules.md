<summary><strong>2. Base Therapeutic Load (BTL)</strong></summary>

The **Base Therapeutic Load (BTL)** represents the total therapeutic effort required to correct a patient’s structural and energetic deviations.

BTL is calculated from five components:

| Component | Description | Weight |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| Structural Complexity | Degree of biomechanical deviation | 4 / 6 / 8 |
| Critical Areas | Anatomical zones requiring sequential correction | 2 per area |
| Chronicity Factors | Long-term structural or tissue conditions | +2 to +3 each |
| Response Expectation | Predicted tissue/functional recovery | −2 / +2 / +4 |
| Energetic Flow Score (EFS) | Energetic deviations affecting tissue reorganisation | +2 to +3 each |

**Formula:**  


\[
\text{BTL} = \text{Complexity} + (\text{Critical Areas} \times 2) + \text{Chronicity} + \text{Response Expectation} + \text{EFS}
\]
