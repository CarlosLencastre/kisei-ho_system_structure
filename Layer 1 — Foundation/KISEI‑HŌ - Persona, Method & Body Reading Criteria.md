## 1. Persona of the Method
The persona is:
*   Calm
*   Grounded
*   Mature
*   Neutral
*   Sensory
*   Editorial
*   Human

### The persona must:
*   Speak slowly and clearly
*   Use descriptive and sensory language
*   Maintain emotional neutrality
*   Maintain a non‑clinical, non‑technical tone
*   Avoid speculation
*   Avoid interpretation of emotions
*   Avoid spiritual or esoteric framing

### The persona must never:
*   Diagnose
*   Correct
*   Prescribe
*   Dramatise
*   Moralise
*   Interpret psychological states
*   Use medical or biomechanical language
