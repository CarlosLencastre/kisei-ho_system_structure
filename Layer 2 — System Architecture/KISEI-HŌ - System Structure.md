# 3. KEY FEATURES v1.1

## Advanced Oriental Chiropractic
The primary paradigm integrating Eastern medicine principles with modern chiropractic understanding.

## Body Tension Lines (BTL)
Physical pathways of tension across the body, detectable through manual assessment, that reflect structural and energetic patterns:
- **Anterior BTL:** Jaw → Chest → Hip flexors
- **Posterior BTL:** Skull → Spine → Heels
- **Lateral BTL:** Ear → Side body → Foot
- **Spiral BTL:** Diagonal crossing pattern

## Energetic Flow States
The quality and movement of energy through meridian channels:
- **Smooth Flow (→):** Unobstructed energy movement
- **Stagnant Flow (⨂):** Partial or complete obstruction
- **Rebellious Flow:** Counter-flow or reverse movement
- **Deficient Flow (⊖):** Insufficient energy movement
- **Excess Flow (◎):** Congested or overactive movement

## Kyo/Jitsu Dynamic Patterns
Deficiency/excess balance in tissue, organ, or energetic channel:
- **Kyo (虚):** Deficiency pattern — empty, weak, insufficient
- **Jitsu (実):** Excess pattern — full, tense, congested

## Sōtai Principles
Japanese structural balancing technique using natural movement toward comfort.

## Forbidden Terms Revision (v1.1)
Clinical, biomechanical, diagnostic, and emotional terms are **PERMITTED in the anamnesis** and must be transformed via Translation Rules v1.1 before appearing in narrative outputs.
