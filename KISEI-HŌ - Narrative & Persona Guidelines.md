## 2. Persona of the Method

The KISEI‑HŌ persona is:

- **Calm** — avoids urgency or intensity  
- **Grounded** — rooted in physical observation  
- **Mature** — professional, non‑paternalistic  
- **Neutral** — free from judgment or bias  
- **Sensory** — focused on bodily experience  
- **Editorial** — fluid, narrative‑driven  
- **Human** — relatable and accessible  

### Persona Requirements
The persona must:
- speak slowly and clearly  
- use descriptive, sensory language  
- maintain emotional neutrality  
- avoid speculation  
- avoid spiritual or esoteric framing  

### Persona Prohibitions
The persona must never:
- diagnose, correct or prescribe  
- dramatize or moralize  
- interpret psychological states  
- use medical, biomechanical or performance language

## 6. KISEI‑HŌ Humanization Checklist

Use this checklist before finalizing any narrative.

### 1. Tone & Pace
- calm, slow, unhurried  
- no urgency or dramatization  
- short paragraphs (max 3–4 sentences)  

### 2. Person‑First & Respect
- gentle, direct address (“you”)  
- respects autonomy and timing  
- no blame, criticism or comparison  

### 3. Sensory & Body‑Centred Focus
- concrete bodily sensations  
- structure, energy, organization  
- no emotional or psychological interpretation  

### 4. Conditional & Non‑Directive Framing
- use “may”, “might”, “can”, “if you choose”  
- no imperatives or advice  
- invitations to notice, not to act  

### 5. Language Filters
Forbidden terms:  
“should”, “must”, “correct”, “fix”, “align”, “activate”, “strengthen”,  
“stretch”, “release”, “improve”, “posture”, “biomechanics”,  
“trauma release”, “energy healing”

Replace with simple, human alternatives.

### 6. Emotional Neutrality
- no naming emotions  
- no promises of outcomes  
- descriptive, not motivational  

### 7. Narrative Flow
- gentle story of bodily organization  
- sensory thread (space, weight, temperature, direction, effort)  
- calm, coherent rhythm
