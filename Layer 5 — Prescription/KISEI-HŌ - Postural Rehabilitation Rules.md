## 2. Base Therapeutic Load (BTL)
The **Base Therapeutic Load (BTL)** represents the total therapeutic effort required to correct a patient's structural and energetic deviations.
BTL is calculated from six components (v1.1):
| Component | Description | Weight ||-----------|-------------|--------|| Structural Complexity | Degree of biomechanical deviation | 4 / 6 / 8 || Critical Areas | Anatomical zones requiring sequential correction | 2 per area || Chronicity Factors | Long-term structural or tissue conditions | +2 to +3 each || Response Expectation | Predicted tissue/functional recovery | −2 / +2 / +4 || Energetic Flow Score (EFS) | Energetic deviations affecting tissue reorganisation | +2 to +3 each || **BTL Tension Factor** (v1.1 NEW) | Body Tension Line involvement and tension level | +1 to +4 |
**Formula:**
BTL = Complexity + (Critical Areas × 2) + Chronicity + Response Expectation + EFS + BTL Tension Factor

### BTL Tension Factor (v1.1 NEW)

| BTL Pattern | BTL Tension Factor |
|-------------|-------------------|
| Mild BTL tension (single BTL) | +1 |
| Moderate BTL tension (single BTL) | +2 |
| Marked BTL tension (single BTL) | +3 |
| Multiple BTL involvement (any level) | +2 to +4 |
| Spiral BTL tension | +3 to +4 |

## 4. Session Options (Dynamic % Rule)

Session options are derived directly from BTL:

| Option | Percentage | Description |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| Minimum Option | 80% of BTL | Essential correction |
| Adequate Option | 100% of BTL | Complete correction |
| Conservative Option | 120% of BTL | Maximum stability |

These percentages are **fixed and universal**.

**BTL, Flow State, and Kyo/Jitsu Integration:**
- Global Kyo patterns → default to Conservative Option (gentler approach)
- Global Jitsu patterns → Minimum Option may be sufficient (focused softening)
- Rebellious Flow patterns → Conservative Option recommended
- Deficient Flow patterns → Conservative Option recommended

## 11. Phase Objectives

| Phase | Structural Objective | Energetic Objective | BTL/Flow State/Kyo-Jitsu Objective | Techniques |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------|-----------------------------------|------------|
| Phase 1 — Intervention & Stabilisation | Stabilise base, release tension | Restore Hara + lymphatic flow | Release BTL tension + soften Jitsu/Stagnant areas + support Kyo | Myofascial, DLM, pelvic QOA |
| Phase 2 — Active Correction & Strengthening | Correct alignment, integrate diagonal | Improve respiratory flow + tonify | Balance BTL + support Flow States + balance Kyo/Jitsu | QOA, Sōtai, Ori |
| Phase 3 — Consolidation & Reinforcement | Reinforce functional alignment | Stabilise energetic tone | Maintain BTL balance + smooth Flow States + balanced Kyo/Jitsu | Exercises, integration |

### Phase-Specific BTL/Kyo-Jitsu Considerations

**Phase 1:**
- Focus on Jitsu areas (softening) if Jitsu-dominant
- Support Kyo areas gently if Kyo-dominant
- Address primary BTL first
- Prioritize Flow State improvement (Stagnant → Smooth, Rebellious → Smooth)

**Phase 2:**
- Balance BTL across all pathways
- Support Kyo areas progressively
- Continue softening Jitsu areas as needed
- Promote Flow State integration

**Phase 3:**
- Maintain BTL balance
- Support global Kyo if present
- Prevent re-accumulation of Jitsu patterns
- Maintain smooth Flow States
