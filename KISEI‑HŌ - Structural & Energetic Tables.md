## 4. Edge Case Validation

### Handling Conflicting Data
If structural and energetic data conflict (e.g., structural data suggests upper‑dominant organisation but energetic data suggests lower‑dominant):

1. Prioritise energetic data.  
2. Cross‑reference Centre, Root and Firmament.  
3. Consult the Energetic Framework.  
4. Document the conflict in the Integrated Analysis.

### Examples of Edge Cases
| Scenario | Resolution |
|----------|------------|
| Structural: Upper‑dominant; Energetic: Lower‑dominant | Prioritise energetic data; note structural tendency. |
| Blockage in Spine + Excess in Shoulders | “Compensatory effort in upper body due to central tension.” |
| Missing data for a region | Omit the region; do not speculate. |

### Validation Checklist
- All classifications align with Structural & Energetic Tables.  
- Conflicts resolved using the above rules.  
- Edge cases documented in the Integrated Analysis.
